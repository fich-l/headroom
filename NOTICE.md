## Projects

### starkdmi/BrightXDR — GPL-3.0
<https://github.com/starkdmi/BrightXDR> · © Dmitry Starkov

- The starting point. Headroom began as a repackaging of BrightXDR with an app icon and a fix for
  its white screen during Space switches and Mission Control.
- `Original/BrightXDR/` is an **unmodified** copy of its sources at commit `17b70ea`, built by
  `build_original.sh` as the reference in the A/B test.
- `Compare/Original/MetalView.swift` is an unmodified copy of its `MetalView.swift`;
  `Compare/main.swift` (`showOriginal()`) copies its window setup.
- Headroom no longer uses BrightXDR's full-screen multiply overlay. The measurements that show why
  are in `README.md`.

### xdr-lift — MIT
<https://github.com/Justaboringname/xdr-lift> · © 2026 Yihan Zhu · read at commit `b9ebadc`
· license: `LICENSES/MIT-xdr-lift.txt`

- The approach Headroom uses: a 1×1 EDR "trigger" window plus a scaled display gamma table, and the
  explanation of why it survives Space switches.
- `Sources/Gamma.swift` follows its trigger window setup (window level `CGShieldingWindowLevel() + 1`,
  collection behaviour, 1×1 top-left placement, periodic redraw of the EDR pixel).

### Illuminate — MIT
<https://github.com/fackelmeyer/illuminate> · © 2026 Jonas Fackelmeyer · read at commit `6d71603`
· license: `LICENSES/MIT-Illuminate.txt`

- Scaling the display's captured transfer table linearly by the gain ("Fix 1", the model Headroom
  ships), and falling back to an identity ramp when the reading is unusable.
- Its README explained the overlay vs. gamma-table trade-offs.
- `Sources/OverlayBoost.swift` (overlay mode for M5 Pro / M5 Max) follows its findings: the M5 Pro/Max
  gamma-table bug and its detection by chip name, the shielding window level for the overlay,
  presenting a few frames per second so WindowServer doesn't drop the overlay's compositing, and
  not excluding a multiply overlay from capture. No code was copied.

### BrightIntosh — GPL-3.0
<https://github.com/niklasr22/BrightIntosh> · read at commit `f6806b8`

- Read as a reference for the gamma-table technique. **No code was copied.**

### Also consulted
- XDR Brightness (adanramzan/XDR-brightness-control-macbook) release notes, which describe the same
  switch from a full-screen overlay to a 1×1 trigger plus gamma table.
- The Lunar FAQ (<https://lunar.fyi/faq>) on gamma-table behaviour.

## How Headroom was made

Headroom was written with **Claude Code** (Anthropic's AI coding assistant, model Claude Opus).
The code, the automated flash test and the analysis were generated by the AI. The project owner
directed the work, tested every version on a MacBook Pro by eye, and chose the final look and levels.
